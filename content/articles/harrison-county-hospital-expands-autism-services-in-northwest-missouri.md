Harrison County Community Hospital (HCCH) in Bethany has expanded access to autism services in northwest Missouri, according to a Missouri Hospital Association (MHA) story published Aug. 17.

## What changed

Working with the University of Missouri-Kansas City (UMKC), the hospital expanded autism assessment and therapy services. It also launched a mobile "Pediatric Autism Wellness Services" (PAWS) trailer to reach families across the region.

## The goal

HCCH says the effort is shortening the time families wait for an autism diagnosis and supporting families across the region.

## Part of a statewide campaign

The story is part of MHA's Hospital Benefit and Public Trust Campaign, which highlights how hospitals improve community health. MHA, based in Jefferson City, represents 138 Missouri hospitals and is collecting examples of hospitals' health improvement efforts.
