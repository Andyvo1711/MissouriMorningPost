Ali Modder, 25, is expanding the family's GJ Fish & Chicken restaurants from South Kansas City across the state line, Startland News reported. A fifth location recently opened in Midtown at 204 W. 39th St., and a sixth is planned for Olathe, Kansas, by mid-November.

## From one customer to five locations

The original GJ opened in 2014 at 6807 Longview Road; the family says the first day brought one customer. Locations followed in St. Louis (early 2024), on Blue Ridge Boulevard (September 2024) and in Kansas City, Kansas (late 2025). The family is from Pakistan, and the name honors their hometown, Gulistan-e-Johar.

## How he funds it

Modder funds expansion with savings, forms a separate LLC for each location and avoids loans. He does not advertise and relies on word of mouth. The five locations employ about a dozen people, including his parents, sister and aunt. Chicken is delivered fresh three times a week, and orders are cooked to order, taking 15 to 30 minutes.

## What's next

Modder plans three more area locations and possibly a new concept. For the Midtown restaurant he hired artist Mia Tobin for a colorful, family-friendly look. He said location is the number one thing for a restaurant.
