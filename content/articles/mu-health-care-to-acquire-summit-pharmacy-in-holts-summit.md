MU Health Care has signed an agreement to acquire Summit Pharmacy in Holts Summit, the system announced Oct. 26. The deal is expected to close in December.

## Transition details

Once the sale closes, Summit Pharmacy will be the 12th community pharmacy in the Mizzou Pharmacy network. The pharmacy, at 140 Northstar Drive, stays open during the transition and reopens as a Mizzou Pharmacy location on Dec. 7. MU Health Care expects to buy the inventory and offer jobs to eligible employees.

## For customers

Patients do not need to take action on their prescriptions, and the phone number stays (573) 826-4151. Customers do not need an MU Health Care provider to use the pharmacy, which will continue to serve patients whose providers belong to other health systems.

## Quotes

Tom Greenlee, executive director of pharmacy services for MU Health Care, said Summit Pharmacy has built strong relationships with its customers over the years. Owner Anthony DeSha said the pharmacy has been proud to serve the Holts Summit community and that finding the right organization to continue that legacy was important to him.
