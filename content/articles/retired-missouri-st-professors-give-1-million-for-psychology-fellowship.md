Retired Missouri University of Science and Technology faculty Dr. Robert L. Montgomery and Dr. Frances M. "Dee" Haemmerlie Montgomery have donated $1 million to create a graduate fellowship in psychology, the university announced Oct. 20.

## What the fellowship supports

The Robert L. Montgomery and Frances M. Haemmerlie Graduate Fellowship in Psychology will support graduate student research in the psychological science department, which offers bachelor's degrees in psychology, a master's in industrial-organizational psychology and, since 2025, a Ph.D. in applied psychology.

## Two careers at S&T

Robert Montgomery joined the university in 1968 as an inaugural faculty member in the new psychology section, chaired the department from 2001 to 2007 and retired in 2014 as professor emeritus. Dee Haemmerlie Montgomery joined in 1978 as the first female tenure-track psychology faculty member and became S&T's first female faculty member named a Curators' Distinguished Professor. She retired in 2017.

## In their words

Haemmerlie Montgomery said that as retirees, they want to leave a legacy that will build the department and the careers of students and faculty. Department chair Dr. Clair Reynolds Kueny called the gift one outstanding example of all the couple has done for the department over the years.
