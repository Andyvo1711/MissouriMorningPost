The University of Missouri ranks No. 95 among national universities in the 2027 U.S. News & World Report Best Colleges rankings, a rise of 44 spots since 2020, the university announced Sept. 22.

## How Mizzou compares

The university's release placed Mizzou ahead of several peers, including the University of Iowa (100), the University of Colorado and University of Tennessee (both 109), Iowa State (116), the University of South Carolina (121) and the University of Kansas (148). Mizzou also ranks among the top 13 Best Value Public Universities and is recognized for veterans' services and for undergraduate programs in business, nursing and psychology.

## Student outcomes and research

First-year retention at Mizzou is 91%, and the graduation rate is 77%, above national averages. More than 95% of graduates are working, continuing their education or serving within six months of graduation.

The university also reported record research expenditures of $553 million, the 12th consecutive year of growth. Time magazine named Mizzou among the top 150 universities worldwide, and Niche ranks it among the top five online colleges in the nation.

## Leaders react

UM System President Mun Choi said the rankings show that "Mizzou's momentum is unstoppable." Provost Matthew Martens said Mizzou's students recognize the extraordinary benefits of its education.
