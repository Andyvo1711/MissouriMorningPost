Hydrangea House, a luxury antique and home goods store with interior design services, has leased the former Brookside police and fire station in Kansas City and plans a soft opening Thursday, Nov. 12, Startland News reported.

## The building

The two-story Tudor-style brick building at 22 W. 63rd St. has 6,265 square feet and had been dark for nearly seven years. Station 29 opened June 1, 1918, and closed in 1978; later uses included two banks and a car dealership. The floral shop Fiddly Fig occupied it until it relocated in early 2020.

## The owners

Jessica Carpenter, an interior designer with more than two decades of experience, co-owns the business with her daughter, Abigail Gaughan, a full-time nurse practitioner. The business began as a hobby in a 160-square-foot booth at the Glenwood Antique Mall in Overland Park just over a year ago and closed that location in mid-October. The team now has seven members, including operations manager David Behnke, who has 40 years of retail experience.

## What's next

A sister brand, The Gilded Fox, will occupy the building's west side with a dark, moody style, while the main lobby will have a Grandmillennial look. Events, classes and possibly showrooms are planned for the upper level in late 2027.
