Missouri added 8,100 nonfarm jobs in July, bringing total employment to about 3,014,900, according to the Missouri Economic Research and Information Center's monthly report published Aug. 20.

## Monthly gains

Private industry gained 6,900 jobs and local government added 1,200. Private service-providing industries gained 6,500 jobs, led by education and health services (+2,900) and professional and business services (+1,100). Manufacturing added 900 jobs, while mining, logging and construction lost 500.

## Year over year

Since July 2025, Missouri has added 29,400 jobs. The largest gains were in private education and health services (+9,200), leisure and hospitality (+8,900) and mining, logging and construction (+6,900). Manufacturing was down 2,800 and federal government employment fell by 4,200.

## Unemployment

An estimated 113,556 Missourians were unemployed in July, down 2,797 from June. The unemployment rate fell four-tenths of a percentage point over the year. The labor force participation rate was 63.3% and the employment-population ratio was 61%, both higher than national rates.

*Source: Jefferson City News Tribune (Aug. 20, 2026), citing MERIC.*
