Gary White, co-founder and CEO of Water.org, has been awarded the 2025 Hoover Medal, which recognizes an engineer whose work has improved humanity through technical achievement and service, Missouri S&T announced Aug. 25. He is a Missouri S&T alumnus.

## From Rolla to a global nonprofit

White began working on water projects in Latin America as an S&T undergraduate. In 1990 he founded WaterPartners, which merged with Matt Damon's H2O Africa Foundation in 2009 to form Water.org. The organization now operates in 15 countries across Asia, Africa and Latin America. He was honored for more than 30 years of work helping people in poverty get safe water and sanitation infrastructure and financing.

## A rare honor

White is the 76th recipient since the first award to Herbert Hoover in 1930 and the second S&T alumnus to receive it, after Mervin J. Kelly, a 1914 physics graduate and Bell Labs president, honored in 1961. The medal is awarded by a board representing five engineering organizations. He will receive it at World Water Week in Stockholm.
