University of Missouri Health Care has applied to the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC) for Magnet designation, the system announced Oct. 30 in a public notice tied to a coming site visit.

## What Magnet means

Magnet Recognition is described as one of the highest credentials a health care organization can achieve. It signals that an organization meets strict, evidence-based standards of nursing excellence. The program recognizes nurses' contributions and supports nurse engagement, interprofessional teamwork, better patient outcomes and lower health care costs.

## How the public can weigh in

Patients, family members, staff and other interested parties may submit written comments about the organization's nursing care and practice through Dec. 5, 2026. Comments go by email to magnet@ana.org or by mail to the Magnet Recognition Program Office in Silver Spring, Maryland. Those who wish to remain anonymous should say so at the top of the letter or email. The notice states that comments remain confidential and are not shared with the organization.
