Children's Mercy Hospital will be in-network for families who buy UnitedHealthcare Affordable Care Act Marketplace plans in the Kansas City metro in 2027, the hospital announced Nov. 10.

## A reversal

In October, the hospital had said it would not be included in any ACA Marketplace plans in 2027. It says it is still talking with other Marketplace health plans about in-network access and will share updates.

## Families caught in the middle

KCTV5 spoke with Kasey and Tim Jackson, self-employed parents who rely on Marketplace insurance and whose son Elijah receives cancer treatment at Children's Mercy. Kasey Jackson called learning at the end of October that coverage would end on Jan. 1 "barbaric."

## Key dates for shoppers

Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs through Jan. 15, 2027. To have coverage begin Jan. 1, families must select a plan by Dec. 15, 2026. Because insurer and HealthCare.gov provider directories are still being updated, the change may not appear right away. The hospital directs families to ChooseChildrensMercy.org to see which plans are in or out of network.
