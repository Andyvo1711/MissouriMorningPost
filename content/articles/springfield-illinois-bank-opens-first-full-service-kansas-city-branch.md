Bank of Springfield, a community bank based in Springfield, Illinois, has opened its first full-service branch in Kansas City, Missouri, at 8622 NE 85th St., according to a regional trade report published Aug. 20. The branch officially opened Aug. 11.

## By the numbers

The Kansas City location is the bank's 17th branch and its third in Missouri, following branches in Clayton and O'Fallon. Austin Fee, vice president of commercial lending, leads the Kansas City team and works with seven other people at the branch. Founded in 1965, the bank has more than $2 billion in assets, according to the report.

## From loan office to branch

The bank opened a loan production office in Kansas City in 2022. CEO Jason Knoedler, who joined the bank in 2006 and was named CEO in April 2024, called the moment exciting for the company and its Kansas City team, noting that four years after opening the loan office the bank is celebrating its first branch in the community.
