Children's Mercy Hospital in Kansas City eliminated 115 positions on Monday, Nov. 2, about 1.2% of its workforce, KCTV5 reported.

## Who is affected

The layoffs mainly hit administrative, leadership and support roles. Separately, the restructuring is moving 473 employees in revenue cycle and scheduling departments to an outside contractor, Ensemble Health Partners. The hospital held a town hall for remaining staff Monday afternoon.

## The hospital's reasoning

A spokesperson blamed rising operating costs and insurance reimbursement rates that have not kept pace. In an internal letter, President and CEO Alejandro Quiroga, M.D., said the hospital had tried position control, voluntary retirement programs and normal attrition before turning to layoffs. He acknowledged that he had said he would not do layoffs and apologized for breaking that pledge, stressing that the cuts were not about performance.

## Independence as a goal

The hospital said it aims to remain one of only 28 independent, freestanding children's hospitals in the country.
