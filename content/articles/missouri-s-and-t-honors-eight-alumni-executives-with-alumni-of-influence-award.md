Missouri University of Science and Technology honored eight graduates with its highest alumni award, Alumni of Influence, at a Nov. 7 ceremony at Hasselmann Alumni House in Rolla. The program began in 2011, and 50 alumni have been honored since.

## Missouri honorees

**Joseph Rupp** of Frontenac earned a 1972 metallurgical engineering degree and spent 45 years at Olin Corporation, retiring in 2017 as executive chair. He led Olin's 2007 move to sell its century-old metals division and create the world's largest chlor-alkali producer.

**Susan Simmons** of St. Louis earned a 1984 chemical engineering degree and an MBA from Washington University in 1985, then spent 30 years at Anheuser-Busch, rising to senior operations leadership at the St. Louis brewery. She retired in 2016.

## Other honorees

Other honorees include John Gibson, former chair, president and CEO of ONEOK; Dennis Jaggi, co-founder of Flatrock Energy Advisors, whose 2025 gift established the Jaggi School of Business; Robert Keiser, former Oryx Energy chair and CEO; Jeff Sheets, retired ConocoPhillips CFO; Stephen Suellentrop, former Hunt Oil president; and Steven Wunning, retired Caterpillar group president.
