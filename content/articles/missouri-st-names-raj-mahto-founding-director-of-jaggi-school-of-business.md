Dr. Raj Mahto has been named founding director of the Dennis and Janet Jaggi School of Business at Missouri University of Science and Technology, the university announced Nov. 9. He begins in January and will also hold the Dennis and Janet Jaggi Endowed Chair of Business.

## A school built on a gift

The school was made possible by a $12.5 million gift from Dennis and Janet Jaggi. It sits within S&T's Kummer College of Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Economic Development.

## Who Mahto is

Mahto comes from the University of New Mexico's Ellis School of Business Leadership, where he is a Regents' Professor and was previously chair of the Finance and Innovation Department. He founded and directed the Parker Center for Family Business and directed the UNM Small Business Institute. He has published more than 75 peer-reviewed papers and is among the world's top 2% most-cited scientists, according to a Stanford University analysis of Elsevier data.

## What leaders say

James Sterling, vice provost and dean of the Kummer College, said the university is thrilled to welcome Mahto. Mahto said he is committed to building a school model distinctive to S&T's STEM-focused, interdisciplinary context.
