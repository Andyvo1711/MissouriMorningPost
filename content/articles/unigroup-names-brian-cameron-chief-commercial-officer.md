UniGroup, the St. Louis-based parent of United Van Lines and Mayflower Transit, formally announced Brian Cameron as chief commercial officer on Monday, Sept. 21. The role is newly created, not a replacement.

## What Cameron will oversee

Cameron will lead marketing, brand strategy, business insights, sales and member success. He has more than 20 years of experience building commercial organizations, most recently as executive vice president and chief marketing officer of Acosta Group. He previously spent more than a decade at Chesterfield-based Premium Retail Services, where he was the first marketing employee, and held earlier roles at Ticketmaster and the American Red Cross.

UniGroup President and CEO John Hartmann said Cameron brings a strong track record of developing commercial strategies that drive growth.

## A company in transition

UniGroup announced in June that Hartmann would succeed Kevin A. Krakora as president and CEO. The company has also moved from its roughly 35-acre Fenton campus to leased office space in Des Peres. UniGroup describes itself as the nation's largest household-goods relocation organization and operates as a cooperative, with its van lines owned by their agents.

The company has not announced local hiring targets, revenue projections or capital investment tied to the appointment.
