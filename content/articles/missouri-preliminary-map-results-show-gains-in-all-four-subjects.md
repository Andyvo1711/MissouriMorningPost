Missouri students posted gains in English language arts, math, science and government on the 2025-26 Missouri Assessment Program (MAP), according to preliminary results presented to the State Board of Education in Jefferson City on Aug. 18.

## The numbers

Sean Bates, director of assessment at the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE), said the state saw overall increases in all four content areas. Among the highlights:

- Sixth-grade math proficiency rose nearly 3 percentage points to 48.9%.
- English II proficiency rose more than 5 points to 58.4%.
- Biology proficiency rose more than 3 points to 40.6%.
- Eighth-grade science proficiency rose more than 4 points to 43.9%.

English language arts improved in every grade except grades 5 and 6. Biology and American government, which had been struggling end-of-course assessments, both showed improvement.

## What gets tested

Students in grades 3-8 take English and math tests annually. Science is tested in elementary, middle and high school, and government is assessed in high school.

## Final results still to come

Interim Commissioner of Education Stacey Preis said the state is seeing steady progress, adding that there is more to do but the results show upward momentum in classrooms. The results are preliminary; DESE says final district and statewide reports will be released after verification later this fall.
