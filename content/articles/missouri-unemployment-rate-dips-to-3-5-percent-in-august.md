Missouri continued to add jobs in August as the state's unemployment rate declined to 3.5%, according to figures from the Missouri Economic Research and Information Center and the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

## The numbers

Nonfarm payroll employment increased by 2,100 jobs during the month. The seasonally adjusted data show 112,415 Missourians unemployed out of a labor force of 3,169,990. The national unemployment rate for August was 4.1%, unchanged from July, so Missouri's rate was lower.

## Later revisions

When the state released September data, August's job total was revised up by 2,200 jobs from the preliminary estimate, and August's unemployed count of 112,415 was used as the baseline for September's change.

## Context

The report followed a strong July, when the state added 8,100 jobs. Taken together, the summer figures show a labor market that was still growing before September's decline.

*Sources: Missouri Economic Research and Information Center; Bureau of Labor Statistics; Jefferson City News Tribune (Oct. 16, 2026).*
