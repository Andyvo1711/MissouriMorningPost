Two Missouri salons are among the honorees in Salon Today's 2027 Salons of the Year design competition, announced Oct. 26.

## The Missouri winners

- **EssereBlu The Salon, The Space**, in St. Louis, owned by Charles and Nicole Gillick.
- **Studio 703**, in Chesterfield, owned by Dee Broderick, Shawn Summers, Emily Dall'Est and Natalie Mantia.

The announcement did not list category or award designations for either salon.

## What comes next

Salon Today said it will later announce a grand prize winner, runners-up, category winners and Salons of Distinction. In the coming months it will publish design details, images and owner videos in a microsite.

*Source: Salon Today (Oct. 26, 2026).*
