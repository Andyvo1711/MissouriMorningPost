Missouri State University's systemwide enrollment is 26,917 this fall, down 1.1% from last fall's record, according to figures the university released Sept. 22.

## Campus-by-campus numbers

The Springfield campus enrolled 25,039 students, with undergraduate enrollment down 0.9% and graduate enrollment down 3.4%. The West Plains campus enrolled 1,878 students. The university welcomed 2,506 first-time students, and retention for that group rose to 76.9%. Dual-credit enrollment continues to grow, and more than 78% of systemwide students are from Missouri.

## Health and education colleges grow

The McQueary College of Health and Human Services is now the university's largest college, up 4.7% to 4,742 students. The College of Education grew 2.3% to 1,644 students and remains the state's top producer of educators.

## What officials say

Dawn Medley, vice president for enrollment management, said overall enrollment is down just over 1% amid rising competition from a shrinking student population. She said the university has launched strategic enrollment management playbooks so the whole campus can contribute to growth. President Richard B. Williams said the focus is meeting students where they are, including first-time freshmen, transfers and adults returning to finish degrees.
