Ernest K. "Ernie" Banks, a manufacturing and operational excellence leader who lives in St. Louis, joined the Missouri S&T Board of Trustees at its Sept. 18 fall meeting in Rolla, the university announced Oct. 19.

## His career

Banks earned a 1981 bachelor's degree in chemical engineering from S&T and a master's from Georgia Tech in 1984. He is director of strategic projects and operational excellence at Orchestra Life Sciences, a technical consultancy for pharmaceutical therapies for cancer and rare disease. He previously worked at Monsanto, Anheuser-Busch and Mallinckrodt/Tyco Healthcare/Curium.

## Community roles

He is a school board member and chair of the operations committee for Lift for Life Academy in St. Louis and a vice president of the Miner Alumni Association.

## Why his experience matters

Board president Michael McEvilly said Banks's experience is critical to the university's ability to fully support it during a pivotal time. Chancellor Mo Dehghani said the university sees opportunities in biomedical research and in preparing students for biotechnology and health care careers.
