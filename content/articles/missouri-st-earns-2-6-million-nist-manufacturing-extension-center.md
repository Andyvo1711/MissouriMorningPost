Missouri University of Science and Technology has been awarded $2.6 million by the U.S. Department of Commerce's National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) to establish a new Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) center, the university announced Sept. 17.

## What the center will do

The funding aims to speed adoption of advanced manufacturing technologies, including artificial intelligence, robotics, automation, biomanufacturing, advanced materials and additive manufacturing. The center will join the MEP National Network, which has nearly 1,400 advisers and experts at more than 450 service locations and helps small and medium-sized manufacturers stay competitive.

The award covers the first year of a five-year cooperative agreement. Later funding depends on availability, performance reviews and alignment with Department of Commerce priorities.

## Built on the Missouri Protoplex

The center builds on the Missouri Protoplex, S&T's advanced manufacturing facility that opened in April. The 116,000-square-foot facility includes 40,000 square feet of high-bay space and more than 60 industrial-scale machines and instruments. S&T has secured more than $40 million to support collaborations with industry and research partners.

## Workforce angle

Chancellor Mo Dehghani called the new center a natural next step. Dr. Richard Billo, director of the Protoplex, said the work will support workforce needs through a statewide training network with Missouri's 12 community colleges.
