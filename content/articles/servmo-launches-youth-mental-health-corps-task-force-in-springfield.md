ServMO, Missouri's state service commission, has launched a statewide initiative to address youth mental health needs, holding the inaugural Youth Mental Health Corps (YMHC) Task Force meeting in Springfield, the Jefferson City News Tribune reported Nov. 6.

## The plan

The task force begins a yearlong planning process to design a customized AmeriCorps program for Missouri. The model recruits Missourians ages 18 to 29 who, once trained, would provide "near-peer" mental health support to children and teens in schools, health care clinics and community organizations.

## What the task force will do

It will identify the state's most pressing youth mental health challenges, select evidence-based interventions and map long-term career and education pathways for members, to help build a sustainable behavioral health workforce.

## Partners and voices

The Schultz Family Foundation and Pinterest are national partners backing the effort. Bennett Boggs, commissioner of the Missouri Department of Higher Education and Workforce Development, said addressing youth mental health requires innovative partnerships and collective action. ServMO Executive Director Brittany Crabtree thanked partners for helping bring the cross-sector task force together.
