Persistent back pain forced young softball player Arielle Rhodes to step away from the game. MU Health Care shared her recovery story Oct. 28.

## The diagnosis

Her pain revealed stress fractures in her lower spine. MU Health Care physicians provided care, and advanced MRI technology tracked her healing.

## The comeback

After months of rest and rehabilitation, Arielle returned to the field pain-free. She hit a home run and earned a spot on the Daniel Boone Little League All-Star team. The team won district and state championships, competed for a place in the Little League Softball World Series and played on ESPN.

## Why it matters

Stress fractures are a reminder that persistent pain in young athletes deserves a medical evaluation. The patient story does not name individual clinicians or provide dates for her injury and recovery.
