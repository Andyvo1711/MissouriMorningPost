Missouri hospitals have made significant progress in community engagement, financial investment and data infrastructure since 2017, but workforce capacity is slipping, according to the Missouri Hospital Association's 2026 Triennial Population Health Assessment Report, released Oct. 22.

## Participation

The survey was completed by 81 hospitals and health systems representing 120 hospitals, an 88.9% completion rate among MHA-member hospitals. Participation has grown by 39 hospitals since the inaugural assessment in 2017.

## What the data show

Between 2017 and 2026, average finance scores rose 200% and data and technology scores rose 40%. Among hospitals that took part in both the 2023 and 2026 assessments, workforce capacity scores fell 6%.

## Opportunities identified

The report points to three areas for improvement: strengthening workforce capacity, improving data sharing and care coordination, and expanding readiness for value-based care.

## Next steps

MHA, a Jefferson City nonprofit representing 138 Missouri hospitals, will use the findings to guide its strategic priorities, advocacy and resources. Participating hospitals will receive individualized reports to support their planning.

*Source: Missouri Hospital Association (Oct. 22, 2026).*
