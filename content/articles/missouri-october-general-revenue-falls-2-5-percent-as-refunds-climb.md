Missouri's general revenue collections fell 2.5% in October compared with October 2025, to $943.1 million from $967.1 million, according to a report from State Budget Director Trevor Foley summarized Nov. 6 by the Jefferson City News Tribune.

## Category movement

- **Individual income tax:** up 0.5% year over year, to $2.58 billion.
- **Sales and use tax:** up 6.7%, to $1.20 billion.
- **Corporate income and franchise tax:** up 35.7%, to $284.3 million.
- **Pass-through entity tax:** down 13.4%, to $89.2 million.
- **All other collections:** down 11%, to $235.8 million.
- **Refunds:** up 8.3%, to $335.7 million.

## What drove the decline

Larger refunds and falling pass-through and other collections more than offset gains in sales and corporate taxes.

## Looking back

Missouri's fiscal year 2026 closed with collections 2.8% below the prior year, which the state's budget watchdogs tied partly to the elimination of the capital gains tax.

*Source: Jefferson City News Tribune (Nov. 6, 2026).*
