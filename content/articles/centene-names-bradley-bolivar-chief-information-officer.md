Centene Corporation, the St. Louis-based health insurer, has named Bradley Bolivar its chief information officer, effective Aug. 31.

## Who he is

Bolivar comes from Fannie Mae, where he joined in 2020 as a cloud infrastructure executive and was promoted to CIO in 2023. Before that, he spent 2005 to 2020 at Warner Bros. Entertainment, according to Healthcare Dive. He succeeds Brian LeClaire, who is retiring by the end of October 2026, as planned.

## The strategy behind the hire

Centene CEO Sarah London said data, technology and AI are not just business enablers but strategic capabilities for the company.

## Other leadership changes

Centene also announced in August that Chief Financial Officer Drew Asher, who has held the role since 2021, will step down at the end of 2026, Healthcare Dive reported.
