MU Health Care is offering flu and COVID-19 vaccinations at events across Central Missouri this fall, including two walk-in events in Jefferson City, the Jefferson City News Tribune reported Sept. 10.

## When and where

The Jefferson City events are Saturday, Sept. 19, from 8 a.m. to noon and Friday, Oct. 2, from 4 to 7 p.m., in the first-floor lobby of the Madison Street Medical Building, 1125 Madison St. Appointments are not required.

## Who can get what

Flu shots are available for ages 6 months and older, COVID-19 vaccines for ages 12 and older, and a high-dose flu vaccine for ages 65 and older. Attendees should bring a photo ID and insurance card.

## More information

A full list of MU Health Care's vaccination events and locations is at muhealth.org/flu.
