Five startups pitched at the 11th Pure Pitch Rally, a Kansas City-area event founded by Karen Fenaroli, and left with more than $58,000 in announced funding plus access to resources and networks, Startland News reported Oct. 27.

## The winners

- **Electravera** (Bri Farris and Shelby Atherton): $20,000. The University of Kansas spinout uses an electrochemical process to recover high-purity lithium and cobalt derivatives and plans to find space outside KU for a pilot reactor.
- **AcuSera** (Zaydan Ahmed, 18): $15,000, plus a $2,500 people's choice award, for a smart wristband that responds to acute stress with biometric monitoring, vibration therapy and guided breathing.
- **RESCUE ID** (Fred Neis): $8,000, for a scannable QR code giving responders emergency medical information.
- **RestaurantMate** (Hemkesh Agrawal): $7,000, an AI purchasing platform for independent restaurants.
- **Enhance Health** (Matthew Leming): $6,000, for BREEZE, a breath test measuring ammonia levels for patients with liver and kidney disease.

## How the money works

Each "Land Shark" investor awarded $1,000 to a startup of their choice, and totals reflect on-site announcements; official totals had not been released. Fenaroli said Year 11 is a powerful milestone.
