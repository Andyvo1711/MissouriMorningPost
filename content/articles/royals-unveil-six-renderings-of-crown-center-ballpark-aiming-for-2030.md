The Kansas City Royals released six new renderings of a planned ballpark at Crown Center on Oct. 15 at a downtown reveal event. Groundbreaking is expected in early 2027, and the stadium is scheduled to open for the 2030 Major League Baseball season.

## The design

Populous, a Kansas City-based global design firm, is architect of record. The entrance features limestone framed by layered light-colored materials, metal and terracotta. A lightweight fabric canopy would shade about 65% of seating in late-afternoon conditions, and the exterior draws on local landmarks including Municipal Auditorium, Union Station and the Liberty Memorial. A right-field entertainment building and a left-field Royals headquarters building would connect to the ballpark through two continuous 360-degree concourses.

## Economic pitch

Brooks Sherman, the Royals' president of real estate and development, said the ballpark will lift the city's profile and help jobs and economic activity. The Crown Center site connects the team to an established downtown destination and provides room for a neighborhood built around baseball. Hallmark, a Kansas City-born company, is a partner in the project.
