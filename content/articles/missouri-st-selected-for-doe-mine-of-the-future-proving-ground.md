The U.S. Department of Energy has selected Missouri University of Science and Technology in Rolla as one of four projects in its "Mine of the Future" effort, a $73 million grant program, the university announced Sept. 14.

## The project

S&T's project, the "Multi-Functioning Mining Technology Proving Ground for Energy Efficiency and Productivity," received $16.3 million. The university will design, build and equip a proving ground that combines underground technology testing with advanced energy comminution assessment. Comminution is the process of crushing rock into smaller pieces for processing.

## Part of a larger critical-minerals push

The Department of Commerce separately awarded $38 million to the Critical Minerals and Materials for Advanced Energy Tech Hub, which S&T leads. That funding will support an 18,000-square-foot critical mineral processing and recycling test bed.

## Why S&T

Dr. Kwame Awuah-Offei, the Union Pacific/Rocky Mountain Energy Professor of mining engineering, said S&T is the perfect fit given its existing Experimental Mine. Dr. David Borrok, vice provost and dean of the College of Engineering, noted that the university's roots are in mining and materials science since its founding in 1870.
