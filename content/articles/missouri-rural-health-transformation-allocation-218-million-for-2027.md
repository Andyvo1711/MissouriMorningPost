Missouri will receive $218,547,090 in 2027 from the federal Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP), according to allocations announced Nov. 9 by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

## The federal announcement

CMS announced $10 billion in second-year RHTP awards to all 50 states. Missouri's allocation is an increase of $2 million over 2026 and the ninth highest nationwide for the second year running.

## State investments

The Missouri Hospital Association said last week Gov. Mike Kehoe and the Department of Social Services highlighted more than $55 million in new and upcoming RHTP funding, including an $11 million Workforce Retention and Enablers opportunity.

## Digital backbone

The state also released details of its Digital Backbone investments, which aim to help rural providers improve interoperability, cybersecurity, data sharing and adoption of advanced technology. The Department of Social Services lists four readiness levels: Foundational (facilities without a certified electronic health record), Modernization, Advanced Technologies (such as ambient AI documentation, remote patient monitoring or telehealth) and Digital Innovation.
