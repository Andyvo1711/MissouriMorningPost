MU Health Care and Ambetter Health, the Health Insurance Marketplace product of St. Louis-based Centene Corporation, have reached an agreement that puts MU Health Care in Ambetter's Marketplace network beginning Jan. 1, 2027, the health system announced Nov. 5.

## What patients get

Eligible Ambetter members will have in-network access to MU Health Care's seven hospitals, more than 80 clinics and a network of primary and specialty care providers. The agreement is intended to help patients use their in-network benefits.

## What leaders said

MU Health Care CEO Ric Ransom said ensuring patients have access to the physicians, specialists and services they need is central to the system's mission. Katherine Kirby, president of Ambetter Health, said the network expansion gives members greater access to quality care close to home.

## Checking coverage

Patients can review accepted insurance plans on MU Health Care's managed care plans webpage.
