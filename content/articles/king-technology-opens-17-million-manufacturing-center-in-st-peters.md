King Technology opened a $17 million manufacturing and distribution center in St. Peters on Sept. 29, with Gov. Mike Kehoe and St. Peters Mayor Len Pagono attending the ribbon cutting.

## Facility and jobs

The company is investing at least $17 million, and the facility is expected to employ about 40 workers within three years. A company award announcement describes a 208,000-square-foot facility, with manufacturing added in phases over time. King Technology is keeping its headquarters in Minnetonka, Minnesota.

## Who helped

Partners on the project included Ameren, the City of St. Peters, the Economic Development Council of St. Charles County, Greater St. Louis Inc., the Missouri Department of Economic Development, Missouri Partnership and Spire. King Technology received a 2026 Business Attraction Project Award for the facility.
