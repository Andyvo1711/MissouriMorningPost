Missouri's general revenue collections rose 4.1% in August compared with August 2025, to $1.07 billion, the Jefferson City News Tribune reported Sept. 9. That was about $30 million more than a year earlier.

## By tax type

Year-to-date, individual income tax rose 3.9%, from $1.23 billion to $1.28 billion, and was up 5.3% for the month. Sales and use tax rose 5.8% to $635.8 million. Corporate income and franchise tax rose 6.1% year to date to $52.5 million, although it was down 22.3% for the month. Pass-through entity tax rose 3.1% to $27.3 million.

## Declines

"All other" collections fell 21.4% year to date, to $107 million. Refunds were down 15.5% year to date, to $145.1 million, which helped net collections.

## Why it matters

General revenue is the money lawmakers use for most of the state's budget. Gov. Mike Kehoe has frozen $2 million lawmakers allocated this year for Mizzou's new energy center, a freeze he can reverse if revenue allows.
