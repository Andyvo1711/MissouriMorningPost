Runners took to the streets of Kansas City on Saturday, Oct. 17, for the 40th Garmin Kansas City Marathon, one of the biggest marathons in the metro, KCTV5 reported.

## The race

More than 11,000 runners were registered this year. The full marathon covered 26.2 miles, and half-marathon, 10K and 5K options were also offered. The race started and finished at Frank A. Theis Park, next to the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art.

## The route

The course passed landmarks including the National WWI Museum and Memorial, Union Station, the Country Club Plaza and the historic 18th and Vine district.

## A running tradition

The race's 40th anniversary reflects the staying power of distance running as a community wellness activity in the region. Official results were not available in the coverage reviewed for this story.
