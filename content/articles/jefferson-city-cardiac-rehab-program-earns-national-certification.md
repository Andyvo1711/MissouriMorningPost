The cardiovascular rehabilitation program at MU Health Care in Jefferson City has earned certification from the American Association of Cardiovascular and Pulmonary Rehabilitation (AACVPR), the health system announced Oct. 15.

## What the program does

Cardiac rehabilitation serves patients recovering from events such as heart attacks or coronary artery bypass surgery, combining exercise, education, counseling and support for patients and their families.

## How certification works

The program submitted extensive documentation of its practices. AACVPR uses an outcomes-based process that measures performance with a focus on meaningful patient outcomes. The certification lasts three years.

## What a cardiologist says

Dr. Sostanie Enoru, an MU Health Care cardiologist, said the certification reflects the commitment the team brings to patients every day, and called it another sign of high-quality cardiac care close to home.
