Natalie Wittenberger, a Jefferson City native, grew up around theater and aimed to become a performer. Today she is a hair stylist who owns the business NCW Hair at Heads Up Salon, the Jefferson City News Tribune reported Oct. 15.

## A winding path

She studied communication at Missouri State University for a year, then switched to musical theater, and later cycled through several majors. Before cosmetology she worked as a local school paraprofessional during the COVID-19 pandemic and taught dance classes.

## Finding cosmetology

A family friend introduced her to cosmetology, which appealed to her for its schedule flexibility, artistic work and the science of cutting and coloring. She started at Merrell University of Beauty Arts & Science in 2021 and graduated a year later. About three years after that, she joined Heads Up Salon, owned by Candi Hill.

## Content in her work

Wittenberger described feeling "content" in a stable, authentic role after years of temporary phases. Starting cosmetology school, she said, felt a little scary but also really exciting.
