JCNT Magazine, a publication of the Jefferson City News Tribune, announced finalists for its inaugural 30 Under 30 Rising Leaders class, drawn from more than 25 Mid-Missouri companies and organizations across a dozen industries. A recognition ceremony was held Nov. 4 at The Millbottom.

## Health, beauty and recreation finalists

Among the 10 finalists profiled were several working in wellness-related fields:

- **Natalie Wittenberger, 29,** a cosmetologist and barber at NCW Hair.
- **Maddie Wilde, 24,** a speech-language pathologist at Sam B. Cook Healthplex, MU Health Care, who earned the hospital's Great Catch award for noticing signs of a stroke.
- **Josie Quintaneiro, 24,** also a speech-language pathologist there, who runs the Parkinson's Support Group.
- **Saxon Teubner, 28,** a dentist who opened Capital City Dentistry with his brother in 2025.
- **James Reinkemeyer, 29,** a program manager at Jefferson City Parks, Recreation and Forestry who oversees about 30 employees and was named the 2026 Special Olympics of Missouri Central Area Coach of the Year.

## How the class was chosen

The public submitted nominations in September, and the magazine's editorial board selected the finalists, who appear in the fall issue.
