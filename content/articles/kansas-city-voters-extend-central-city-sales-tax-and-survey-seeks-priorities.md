Kansas City voters approved a 10-year extension of the Central City Economic Development (CCED) Sales Tax in August with 69.2% support, and the Economic Development Corporation of Kansas City (EDCKC) is now asking residents where the money should go next, Startland News reported Oct. 27.

## About the tax

The one-eighth-cent tax covers the Central City District, from 9th Street to Gregory Boulevard and Indiana Avenue to The Paseo. Since voters first approved it in 2017, it has approved more than $97 million across eight funding rounds for 68 projects representing more than $1 billion in total development activity. It works as gap financing alongside other funding sources.

## The survey

EDCKC's 10-minute Community Vision Survey is open through Dec. 18 and will inform a 2027-2032 strategic plan. It asks about housing needs, commercial redevelopment, desired businesses and a vision for 2032.

## Projects it has backed

The city council has approved more than $8 million for 10 projects representing more than $50 million in investment, targeting 210 housing units, home repairs, historic restoration and commercial development. Funded projects include the Zhou B. Art Center, One Nine Vine and Offices at Overlook.
