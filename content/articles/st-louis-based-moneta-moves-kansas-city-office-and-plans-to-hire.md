Moneta Group Investment Advisors, a St. Louis-headquartered, partner-owned registered investment advisor with more than $50 billion in assets under management, moved its Kansas City-area office to a new location in Mission Woods, Kansas, which opened Oct. 29.

## The new office

The office at 1957 W. 51st St. has 25 employees and room for 19 more. Moneta said it plans to hire 8-10 employees in Kansas City over the next 12 to 24 months. The office opened in 2020, doubled in size by 2022, and this is the firm's third office move in the area. It is designed as a smaller version of the St. Louis headquarters at The Plaza.

## How Moneta has grown

Moneta's first expansion beyond St. Louis was Denver in 2019. Kansas City followed in 2020, Boston in 2021 and Chicago in 2022, with a second Denver team in 2023 and Boulder in 2024. In September 2025, Lane Hipple Wealth Management Group joined, extending the firm to Philadelphia and New Jersey.

## Leaders' comments

CEO Eric Kittner said that when Moneta enters a market, it commits to it. President and COO Keith Bowles said the new address signals the firm is there for the long term. Steven Diederich, a Kansas City-based partner who grew up in the area and helped launch the office, said the goal was to build something that would last.
