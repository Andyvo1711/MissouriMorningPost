Missouri lost 10,600 jobs in September, and the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate rose 0.1 percentage point to 3.6%, the Jefferson City News Tribune reported Oct. 16.

## Where jobs were lost

Nonfarm payroll totaled 3,007,300. Private service-providing industries lost 11,300 jobs. Professional and business services fell 5,200, private education and health services 4,200, trade, transportation and utilities 2,600, and financial activities 1,400. Federal government employment fell by 4,100. Leisure and hospitality (+1,400) and manufacturing (+600) posted gains.

## Unemployment

An estimated 113,644 Missourians were unemployed, up 1,229 from August. The rate was down 0.3 points from September 2025, when it was 3.9%, and below the national rate of 4.2%.

## The bigger picture

From September 2025 to September 2026, Missouri gained 28,400 jobs. The employment-population ratio was 61.1%, 1.9 percentage points above the national figure of 59.2%.

*Source: Jefferson City News Tribune (Oct. 16, 2026).*
