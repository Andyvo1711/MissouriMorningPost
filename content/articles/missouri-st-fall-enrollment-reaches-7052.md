Official fall 2026 enrollment at Missouri University of Science and Technology is 7,052, recorded at the end of the semester's fourth week, the university reported Oct. 1.

## Breakdown

The total includes 1,176 first-time freshmen, 273 transfer students, 493 new graduate students, 714 extended-learning students and 6,338 on-campus students. The most popular undergraduate majors are mechanical engineering, aerospace engineering, computer science, electrical engineering and civil engineering. The largest graduate programs are engineering management, civil engineering and computer science.

## New programs and the business school

S&T has added 13 programs over the past three academic years, including bachelor's degrees in biochemistry, biomedical engineering, data science and semiconductor engineering, and master's degrees in applied artificial intelligence and geospatial engineering. The inaugural class of the Dennis and Janet Jaggi School of Business has 45 students, each of whom received a $1,000 scholarship.

## Other figures

A total of 124 students work full time through the Cooperative Education Program, and 258 students take classes at Missouri State University's Springfield campus through the two universities' Cooperative Engineering Program, up 50 from 2025. Chancellor Mo Dehghani said S&T is preparing students for careers that may not yet exist.
