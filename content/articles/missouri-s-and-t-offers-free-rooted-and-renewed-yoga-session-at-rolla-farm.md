Missouri S&T students, faculty and staff can attend one free "Rooted & Renewed" session at Moss & Maple Farm in Rolla, the university announced Oct. 5 on its eConnection site.

## What it is

Rooted & Renewed is a one-hour wellness experience combining gentle yoga, guided meditation, journaling and reflection. No experience is needed, and journals and infused water are provided.

## When and where

Sessions run from 5:30 to 6:30 p.m. on Wednesdays at 10300 County Road 8130 in Rolla.

## How to claim it

Participants use the code MINERSWELL on the Moss & Maple Farm website to get a first session free.
