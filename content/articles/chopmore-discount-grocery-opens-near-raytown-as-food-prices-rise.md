Cosentino's Food Stores soft-opened ChopMore! By Price Chopper, a no-frills discount grocery, in the former Price Chopper at Brywood Center, 8700 E. 63rd St. near Raytown. A grand opening and ribbon cutting was set for Oct. 27, Startland News reported.

## How it saves money

Products are priced at cost, with 10% added at checkout to cover overhead. The store cuts costs through no marketing, ads or loyalty cards, bin-style displays and pallet drops on endcaps. Executive Vice President John Cosentino promised shoppers will save money on every visit. The store now employs about 60, down from about 100 at the former Price Chopper; Cosentino said the reduction occurred through attrition with no layoffs.

## Market context

The USDA Economic Research Service projects food prices will rise 2.9% in 2026 and 2% in 2027. A June McKinsey & Company report describes a "value now" consumer who is more selective with spending.

## The company's plans

Overland Park-based Cosentino's owns 31 stores and employs about 4,000 people. It plans more than $40 million in remodels and new stores this year and next, including a new Pleasant Hill Price Chopper in 2027 and possibly more ChopMore! locations.
